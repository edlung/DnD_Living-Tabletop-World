# hollow-choir, round 2: binding decisions after the final critic's flight simulation

Source: `finale-sim/results.md` (400 flights per start, key and table size). Apply in every place
the flight is stated: encounter `six-strokes` (terrain/trigger/objective/tactics/scaling),
handouts `stair-card` and `stair-dm`, evening 6 scenes and dmNote, item `clock-key`, `world.json`
endings and truth where they summarise the flight. Keep `stair-card` and `stair-dm` ≤ 1900 chars and
the 8-player package ≤ 500 000 bytes.

1. Entry without the key: «в начале своего хода раунда 2 человек стоит в рядах 12–14 и ходит полным
   пределом» — one wording in all places.
2. Starting places: before round 1 the table places its people freely in rows 12–14 (up to 8 per
   row); the map's tokens are only default places. Servants and the Gatekeeper stand where the map
   shows them, at the gate: rows 1–2 (not 2–3).
3. Candles at 06:00: the `stair-dm` examples state the candle count per table (cautious: 12 spare + 8
   Urm; middle: 8 + 4; noisy: 4 + 0) and who is usually left (cautious: Egor taken; middle: Egor and
   Dorota; noisy: Egor, Tadeush, Dorota, Evelina). New rule: a person in row 1 may light a candle
   from the fire of anyone who has already passed the gate (bonus action).
4. Irina carried moves at most 2 rows per round, whoever carries her (keeps a race for the cautious
   table).
5. Choir's call: «провал — предел на 1 меньше на этот раунд; у ведомого или несомого — у всей
   группы».
6. Release: the released person moves 2 rows towards the gate and does not move further that round;
   releasing requires a burning candle in hand and spends it.
7. Remove «Туманный шаг… мимо слуги» (servants do not block squares); Misty Step stays capped by the
   3-row limit.
8. `stair-dm` names the stats line of the servants (ghoul) and the Gatekeeper (ghast: AC 13, 36 HP,
   bite +5 1d8+3, claws +5 2d6+3) and says ranged attacks at him are allowed.
9. The round-6 tie-break in `world.endings` equals the encounter's.
