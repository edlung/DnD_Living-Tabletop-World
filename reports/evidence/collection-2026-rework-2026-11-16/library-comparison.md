## Library-wide repetitions

1. **A pencil track on a handout, with every change said aloud with number and reason** (14/15). It is usually read through named threshold bands.
2. **Exactly three endings**, each with a cost line, graded win-at-a-price / partial / loss (14/15). The shared tone is "it stands, on someone else's blood" (ember-crown says this literally).
3. **The finale is a map encounter with a bespoke rule.** A finale card is filled in aloud before round 1, and the fight is capped at 6–8 rounds (12/15).
4. **Rhythm:** 8 evenings of "three 50-minute scenes plus a 15-minute break" (near-verbatim in 5 campaigns). The final evening runs short–long–short. In 8 campaigns, one evening is one leg, day, district or step.
5. **Beat placement:** a document reveal around evening 5, testimony in evening 7, the finale in evening 8.
6. **Deadlines counted in strokes, bells or hours** (8 campaigns).
7. **Boilerplate table guidance:** a no-roll path for every check; "6 = three pairs, 7 = two pairs and a trio, 8 = four pairs"; seats 7–8 "full but nothing depends on them"; an absent-hero paragraph; a pre-game consent question about a hero-cost.
8. **Backstory dated "N days before evening 1"**, often with "forty".
9. **Closing images that count or name people aloud** (3 campaigns).

## Verdict

**Yes.** One HIGH cluster involves a reworked campaign: **ash-testament × rain-check**. Both are witness-protection plots with a pursuers' gap track, a fleeing witness, testimony in evening 7, a grid fight in evening 8 to keep his token safe, and alive / taken / dead endings. The other HIGH cluster (marrow-crown × salt-widows) contains no reworked campaign. The other six reworked campaigns appear only in MEDIUM or LOW clusters. The strongest of those are the evening-5 "suppressed text read aloud" device, which velvet-ledger and salt-meridian sharpen with the shared "40 days", and the headcount evacuation finale with a "count us aloud" close in hollow-choir and winter-caravan.
