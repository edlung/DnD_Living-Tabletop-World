## Result

Chapter 10.2 is now **«Город, который не помещается в тележку»**. Players work with a stage
crew's oversized seven-window screen on a dry loading platform. They can test a paper model,
propose another workable route, or choose among three concrete outcomes:

- Roll the intact canvas and carry it on the first permitted pedestrian crossing. Today's show
  uses a low banner without moving windows; the labeled frame waits for the next freight service.
- Separate the agreed window sections. Three small stands arrive with working windows, but the
  panorama needs a new arrangement and its seams will later need repair.
- Keep the screen intact. The wide direct passage opens after the tide, in two hours; the meeting
  can proceed without the screen, or the opening can move to tomorrow.

The choice changes the actual festival in chapter 11. It does not become an invisible cost or
another sequence of rolls until someone fails. Ordinary inspection, packing and waiting work
without a roll. Only an explicitly chosen rushed operation risks a named repairable edge.
Refusing to help leaves the work with the crew and does not reduce access to the meeting.

The people's route to the dry niche stays open. The Pearl remains in its actual custody, including
offsite custody; no solution uses it as a weight, payment or material. If this optional episode
is skipped, damage and delay do not appear retrospectively.

The obsolete **«Перепутанный маршрут»** encounter, both route automata and its generated battle
map were removed. The two unrelated optional encounters remain. Book counts, native entities,
tokens, the art-binding manifest, revised handout and all five packages now agree. All 34 selected
illustrations and their original/runtime/provenance files are byte-identical; no art generation
or editing was needed. Acorn, Lighthouse and the initial Moon evidence were preserved.
