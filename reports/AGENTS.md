# Stage Reports

Historical stage completion reports, one per stage (`STAGE_<NNN>_COMPLETION.md`). They are immutable records of a past revision, not specifications.

- Do not load this directory wholesale; open only the report for the stage you need.
- Gate handoffs, Astra reviews, and remediation artifacts live in `handoffs/`, `reviews/`, and `remediation/` per `CHECKPOINTS.md`, not here.
