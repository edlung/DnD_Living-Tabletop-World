# Знамя размером с ладонь

Read `README.md` and `scripts/fantasy-campaigns/CONTENT_CONTRACT.md` before editing.
Own only this campaign directory. `source/` contains original authored Russian content;
query individual JSON fields instead of loading generated packages or books.
`world.truth`, NPC secrets/knowledge, clue statements, unlock conditions and session guidance
are DM-private. Player books may contain only safe world/hero material and separately released
handouts. Prepared outcomes are conditional, never executed world facts.
Eight sessions are split two per file. Preserve all six party sizes and the viable first-three
hero core; additional heroes, specific classes, successful checks and optional combat are never
plot gates. Keep each source JSON under 500 lines and every prose string under 1800 characters.
Generated packages, variants and HTML are owned by the parent integration task; do not edit them.
Plot boundary: residents already chose their destinations and have an ordinary safe exit.
The campaign concerns transporting a finished fragile craft display, its authors' permission,
Ten's openly competing packing method, reliable inter-scale signals and earned working alliances.
Keep endings about executed craftsmanship, visible changes and fulfilled promises; do not restore
housing allocation, refugee resettlement, person-versus-box dilemmas or a hidden magical machine.
