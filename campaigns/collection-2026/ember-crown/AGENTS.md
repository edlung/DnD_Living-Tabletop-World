# Корона Последнего Угля

Scope: this directory only. Source contract: `scripts/campaign-collection/CONTENT_CONTRACT.md`.
Original Russian military fantasy for 6–8 players and DM, 16+, eight sessions.
Read `README.md`, then only needed source keys. Do not load generated packages wholesale.
`source/world.json` separates public premise from DM truth; cast secrets, clue statements,
unlock conditions and sessions are DM-private. Player handout text is safe only after release.
The crown is ordinary iron. The enemy is a culpable mortal conqueror, not a hidden guardian.
Keep the city unoccupied until the played finale; no cursed infrastructure or sentient fuel.
Battles are fictional scenes adjudicated by the DM with existing level-3 rules. No siege engine,
automatic army turns, future outcome, activation, illustration or human playtest is implied.
Generated books, packages, variants and maps are owned by shared collection tooling.
