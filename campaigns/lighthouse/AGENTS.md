# Маяк Между Ветрами

Scope: `work-orders/FAMILY_CAMPAIGN_2026-11-08.md`. `README.md` is the entry point. The party is
three players plus one adult DM; a fourth character is an alternative, not an automatically
controlled companion. Read session chapters individually. DM chapters contain major spoilers.

`source/` holds editable structured content. `package.json` is a generated native CampaignPackage;
rebuild it through the pack command, never hand-edit the generated copy. `assets/manifest.json`
records actual generated files, usage, reveal timing and hashes; `assets/AGENTS.md` controls art.
Player materials must not contain hidden motives, solution keys or future outcomes. Campaign
chapters are prepared possibilities; no player choice, discovery or victory is pre-recorded.
