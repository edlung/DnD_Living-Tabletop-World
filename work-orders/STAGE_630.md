# Work Order — Stage 630: The player's companion and online play

Authority: owner approval 2026-11-02 (`reviews/OWNER_FULL_GAME_2026-11-02.json`), program
`docs/FULL_GAME_PLAN.md` (Milestone A, decisions 4, 10, 13 and 14); ADR 0044.

## Deliver

1. **Table talk** (630a): the table's chat — to the table, a word to the DM, the DM's word to one
   player; in character or out of it; the table's rolls in its log; unread marked on the dock.
2. **The companion's alerts** (630b): a clear alert when the table waits for this player — their
   turn, a check the DM asked for, a rest, a reaction, something the DM showed them — that stays
   until it is answered, with a sound and a vibration where the device has one, a count in a
   background tab's title, and the screen kept awake during a session; the DM's screen shows whom
   the table waits for; a reload comes back to the open question.
3. **Private and physical dice** (630c): a roll between the DM and one player; a campaign option
   for real dice whose faces a player enters, marked as entered, every outcome still the server's.
4. **Handouts and the shared view** (630d): the DM shows one player or the party a picture or a
   text; online, every screen shows what the DM shows on the table's screen.
5. **Several characters, summoned creatures** (630e): the DM gives one player several characters
   and the companion switches between them, each keeping its own knowledge; a player runs the
   creatures they summon or command in a fight.
6. **The first-session guide** (630a): how to play, in five short pages from the top bar.

## Boundaries

No voice (Stage 695); no handout library or random tables (Stage 680); no new spells — a summoned
or commanded creature is one the DM adds to a fight and hands to a player. Migrations are additive.
