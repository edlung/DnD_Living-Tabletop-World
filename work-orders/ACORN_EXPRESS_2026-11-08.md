# Экспресс «Тысяча Желудей» — second complete family campaign

Authority: the owner's active goal, «Сделай еще одну захватывающую кампанию полтностью».
The earlier party preferences persist: one adult DM and three players, including ages 8 and 14;
wonder, mysteries, humour and an affecting ending. This is a separate original adventure, not
an expansion that requires playing Lighthouse first.

## Authorized delivery

- Twelve developed evenings with a beginning, escalating mystery, meaningful choices, climax
  and personal epilogues; four new selectable heroes and three active player seats by default.
- A new woodland railway world, distinctive NPCs, actionable clues, alternative paths,
  staged hints, optional balanced encounters, items, handouts and usable maps/tokens.
- A complete illustrated local DM book and player-safe entry/cards; original raster assets,
  coherent visual direction, compact runtime copies and honest provenance/hashes.
- A native CampaignPackage, all party variants, real isolated compiler and World Engine play
  checks, existing workshop import/activation and optional exact-package curated art transfer.
- Small integration fixes only when demonstrated necessary; regression, layout/accessibility,
  editorial/adversarial checks, final report, file identity and handoff.

## Boundaries

Keep the delivered Lighthouse pack and historical evidence intact. Reuse existing application
capabilities; no invented AI jobs, pre-played outcomes or direct canonical-state writes. NPC and
player knowledge stay isolated. Activation and consequential runtime changes retain DM authority.
No production dependencies, deployment/publication, credentials, paid project provider calls,
external messages or real campaign activation. The requested original art uses built-in imagegen.
The session authors and verifies the work; no delegated writer/reviewer or human acceptance claim.
This goal does not authorize a new numbered product stage.
