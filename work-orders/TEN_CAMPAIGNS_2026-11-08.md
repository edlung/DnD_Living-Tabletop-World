# Ten original campaigns for larger tables

Authority: owner request of 2026-11-08: create ten additional campaigns in different genres,
for more players, including several for audiences aged 21+, using a dedicated GPT-6 Astra
agent at max effort for each campaign. This explicit assignment overrides older one-model
defaults for this task. Agents own separate content directories; the session integrates and verifies.

## Delivery

Owner steering during implementation: «Главное чтобы сюжеты небыли похожими с другими
сессиями». Plot distinctness is a primary acceptance criterion. Compare actual central conflict,
revelation, player activity and climax against lighthouse, acorn-express and moon-pearl, and
against the other nine new campaigns. Genre/setting/name changes alone are insufficient.
Avoid repeating their broken world-machine restoration, sentient resource liberation or
stolen museum artifact/auction/erased collective-authorship plots. Record a distinctness matrix.

- Ten original Russian campaigns under `campaigns/collection-2026/`, eight developed sessions
  each, eight selectable heroes, five to eight or six to eight players plus one DM.
- Four clearly marked 21+ campaigns with mature social/psychological themes and adult casts.
- Playable scenes, clues with fallback routes, NPC knowledge, branching consequences, endings,
  handouts, table management and encounter scaling for every supported party size.
- Native CampaignPackage files and party-size variants, offline DM/player books and tactical
  diagrams, shared assembly and verification under `scripts/campaign-collection/`.
- Readiness through the existing isolated compiler, knowledge-boundary checks, meaningful play
  checks, layout audit, report and handoff. Human playtesting is not inferred.

## Boundaries

Preserve all other sessions' work, especially lighthouse, acorn-express and moon-pearl.
No runtime application changes unless separately necessary and justified; no production dependencies,
deployment, publication, credentials, real activation or paid project-provider calls.
No pre-executed future outcomes. The World Engine and DM retain their existing authority.
Deliver authored text and functional vector maps; no claim of generated raster illustrations.
This is content delivery, not a new numbered product stage.
