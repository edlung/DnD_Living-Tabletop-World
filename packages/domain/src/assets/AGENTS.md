# Assets (`src/assets/`)

Asset identity and lineage: requests for images of world entities, the external jobs that run them, the assets they produce, and each entity's canonical reference. Authority: `docs/adr/0022-asset-identity.md` (owner-approved triggers, approval, and visibility), ADR 0006 §9 (external work and undo), and `docs/adr/0035-images.md` §4 (purposes). Gate 05 reviewed this surface (`docs/GATE_COVERAGE.md`, Stages 150–160); Gate 06 reviews the Stage 330 additions (row 330).

Rules:

- Visual state is not world state (invariant 14). No asset event writes a world fact, and no gameplay decision reads the lineage. `lineage.test.ts` compares every world fact before and after.
- Stage 440 `AssetAdopted` is confined to a USER-confirmed bootstrap batch, followed
  by normal initial approval. It creates no request or provider job. Its `origin`
  survives supersession, retraction and undo restores; it is DM lineage only, never
  a player projection. Replay and integrity bind derived assets to the chosen
  reference bytes and the final campaign bootstrap source.
- A battle map's grid (Stage 670b, docs/adr/0057-encounters-maps-loot.md §3): `AlignBattleMap` (DM) records `BattleMapAligned` on a candidate or approved battle map only — a cell's size, its first full cell's corner in the picture's pixels, its columns and rows — on the asset record (`grid`), shown to players with the picture (`playerAssetView`); undo restores the record (`undo.ts` `assetRestores`, which every undoable picture event goes through). Integrity: a grid on a battle map only. Visual state, never a world fact: a fight takes the grid's size only through the DM's start.
- The DM's uploaded battle maps go through the ordinary lineage under command IDs `<campaign>.upload.<request>` (request and approval by the DM, start and completion by the server for the worker, generator `upload`): the running worker never starts them (`apps/server/src/images/own-requests.ts`).
- The worker's reports (`StartAssetJob`, `CompleteAssetJob`, `FailAssetJob`) are system-only. They, `CancelAssetRequest`, and `RetractAsset` are final: not undoable, folded as recorded by the re-decision oracle, and reading no fact a world command wrote. Keep it so, or a failed job would block undo forever.
- Undo safety for external work lives in `hold.ts`, not in footprints: a started job, or an image neither retracted nor discarded, blocks undo of the command that requested it.
- A job copies its request when it starts, so lineage survives an undo of the request. Never make job events require the request.
- Players see only `SHOWN_ASSET` statuses with audience `players`, of entities the perspective knows or perceives, as variant, purpose, content hash, and media type (`playerAssetView`; no version since Gate 06: it counted hidden pictures). Players are served only through `../visual/views.ts` (`entityAssetsAt`, `assetContentAt`: what presentation could choose; Gate 06); this file's `dmEntityAssets` and `dmAssetContent` are the DM's, take no perspective, and are not exported from the package. Anything else is `null` (a 404), the same as a nonexistent entity.
- Purposes (ADR 0035 §4): `image` (default), `figure` (character, NPC, creature) and `battle-map` (location); a figure or battle map is a derived variant of the canonical reference. A payload carries `purpose` only when it is not `image` (`purposeField`), so an image is written exactly as before purposes existed and old histories re-decide to the same events; the fold reads an absent purpose as `image`. Never write `purpose: 'image'` into a payload.
- Players see a purpose with the other safe fields; visibility and the undo hold do not depend on it. Presentation (`../visual/`) chooses images among `image` assets only.
- Approving a picture supersedes the approved pictures of its kind (ADR 0035 amendment "an approval supersedes its kind"; `sameKind`: the same entity and purpose, and for an image the same label, so any two figures or battle maps of an entity are one kind). A reference supersedes the canonical reference (`previous`); a derived picture lists what it superseded (`supersedes`, absent when nothing). Fold, footprint and undo restores cover every superseded record, so a later approval of the kind blocks undo of the earlier one and undo puts it back. `AssetApproved` is version 3 (`../kernel/event-versions.ts`): version 2 approvals (before the rule) are canon as recorded and may leave two of a kind approved; never re-decide them under the rule. After any history the current writer makes, each kind has at most one approved picture (the property tests check it after every step).
- Tests: `lineage.test.ts` (identity, versions, variants, permissions, no world facts), `undo.test.ts` (queued, in-flight, completed, approval), `views.test.ts` (isolation), `purposes.test.ts` (purposes through the lineage, payloads, versions, undo, views), `supersede.test.ts` (an approval supersedes its kind: shown to every perspective, payloads, undo order, retraction, DM-only), `assets.property.test.ts` (randomized, with integrity, the oracle, replay and one approved picture per kind), `purposes.property.test.ts` (random figures, battle maps and wrong-kind refusals, with presentation checked per perspective after every step and the approved picture shown for its kind after every approval); the lineage written before purposes in `packages/combat/src/asset-history.test.ts` (`fixtures/history/5da1b05-asset-lineage.json`) and the approvals written before an approval superseded its kind in `packages/combat/src/approval-history.test.ts` (`fixtures/history/80d06df-approved-kinds.json`).
